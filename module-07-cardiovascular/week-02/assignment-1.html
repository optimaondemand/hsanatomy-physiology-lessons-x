📖 READING FOCUS
Reading 7.05 — External Heart Anatomy
Before completing this assignment, read Reading 7.05 and complete the activities in Lesson 7.05.
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
Six vessels connect the heart to the rest of the circulation. Knowing which connects to which chamber, in what direction, and carrying which kind of blood is the spine of cardiac anatomy. Today you commit that spine to memory using your own labeled diagram.
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
Submit your great-vessel ID sketch with all six vessels named and attached to the correct chamber, both “rule-breaker” vessels marked, and dashed lines used for any vessel not actually visible from the anterior view.
2
Write a 150–200 word reflection on the VR sculpt: what did you understand after placing the heart on the figure that you did not understand from the reading? Be specific about one or two things (e.g., how slanted the heart actually is in the chest; how close the auricles sit to the great vessels).
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
All six great vessels named on the sketch with correct chamber attachment.
☐
Both rule-breaker vessels correctly identified, and dashed lines correctly mark any vessel not visible from the anterior view.
☐
Reflection names a specific spatial insight from VR.
