Reading 7.07 — The Cardiac Cycle

✏️ YOUR DRAWING SESSION THIS WEEK
What to draw. On a fresh copy of your cross-section from Lesson 7.06 (redraw it lightly, or lay tracing paper or a new slide layer over it so your 7.06 drawing stays clean), draw the conduction pathway in a distinct bright color (yellow works well):
• SA node — small dot in the upper right atrial wall near the SVC.
• AV node — small dot in the floor of the right atrium near the tricuspid valve.
• Bundle of His — thin line running down the upper septum.
• Left + right bundle branches — the line splits halfway down the septum.
• Purkinje fibers — fanning out across the inside of each ventricle from the apex upward.
Goal. Build a visible “tree” of yellow inside your heart that marks the path of every heartbeat.
