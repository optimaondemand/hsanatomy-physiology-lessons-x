📖 READING FOCUS
Reading 7.08 — Coronary Circulation
Before completing this assignment, read Reading 7.08 and complete the activities in Lesson 7.08.
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
A cardiologist’s daily work is reasoning from where a coronary artery is blocked to which myocardium is at risk. In this assignment you do that reasoning with a case-style prompt.
🎯 CASE SCENARIO
A 58-year-old patient arrives at the ER with crushing central chest pain, sweating, and nausea. ECG shows ST elevation in the anterior leads. Cardiac catheterization reveals a complete occlusion in the proximal LAD with no other significant disease.
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
Submit your coronary map sketch from Lesson 7.08 with LAD, circumflex, RCA, and great cardiac vein labeled.
2
Submit photos (or exports) of your anterior + posterior drawings showing the coronary tree on the heart.
3
Write a 200–250 word response to the case scenario. Specifically: (a) what region of myocardium is at risk, (b) why this occlusion is sometimes called the “widow-maker,” (c) what conduction-system consequences might also occur, and (d) why time-to-treatment matters (i.e., what is happening to muscle cells minute by minute).
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
Coronary map sketch correctly labels all three arteries + great cardiac vein.
☐
Two views clearly show the coronary tree.
☐
Case response addresses all four sub-questions specifically.
