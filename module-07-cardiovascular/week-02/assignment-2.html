📖 READING FOCUS
Reading 7.06 — Inside the Heart
Before completing this assignment, read Reading 7.06 and complete the activities in Lesson 7.06.
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
The most visually striking fact inside the heart is the gap in wall thickness between the right and left ventricles. That gap is not arbitrary — it is the answer to a pressure problem. In this assignment you submit your internal-heart sketch, your full-circuit blood trace, your two VR snapshots, and a written argument that traces the wall-thickness difference back to a specific functional requirement.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
Why is the left ventricle’s myocardium three to five times thicker than the right ventricle’s? Argue from pressure, distance, and resistance, not just “it has to work harder.”
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
Submit your labeled cross-section sketch from Lesson 7.06 with all four chambers, the septum, all four valves with cusps, and chordae tendineae + papillary muscles for the AV valves. Add directional flow arrows.
2
Submit your full-circuit blood trace — the toe-to-brain list with all six great vessels, all four chambers, and all four valves named in order, with O / D labels at every step.
3
Submit your two VR snapshots: one from inside the right atrium looking at the tricuspid valve, one from inside the left ventricle looking at the aortic valve.
4
Write a 250–350 word response to the focus question. Engage pressure (the systemic circuit operates at ~120/80 mmHg vs. ~25/8 mmHg for the pulmonary), distance (head to toe vs. heart to lungs), and resistance (long branching systemic arteries vs. short, low-resistance pulmonary vessels).
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
Cross-section shows the four chambers, all four valves named correctly, and a visibly thicker left ventricular wall.
☐
Blood trace has 16+ structures (vessels, chambers, and valves) in correct order with accurate O/D labels.
☐
VR snapshots show the requested viewpoints from inside the heart.
☐
Written response engages all three frames (pressure, distance, resistance) with specific numbers and reasoning.
