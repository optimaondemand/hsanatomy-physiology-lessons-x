Module thread — Service. The first arteries to branch off the aorta turn around and feed the heart. The body’s most generous organ is fed first — by itself.
📖 TODAY’S FOCUS
Reading 7.08 — Coronary Circulation
Before you start the activities below, read Reading 7.08 end-to-end, including the contemporary research callout on LVADs.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to name the three named coronary arteries (LAD, circumflex, RCA) and the region of the heart each supplies.
2
I will be able to explain why the coronary arteries fill during diastole rather than systole.
3
I will be able to describe what a heart attack is at the level of artery, plaque, and myocardium.

🥽 YOUR VR SESSION THIS WEEK
What to sculpt. On the external surface of your sculpted heart, trace and sculpt:
• Left coronary artery — emerging from the left side of the aortic root, splitting into:
– LAD traveling down the front interventricular sulcus toward the apex.
– Circumflex wrapping around the left side and back of the heart in the coronary sulcus.
• Right coronary artery — emerging from the right side of the aortic root, traveling in the coronary sulcus around to the back.
• Great cardiac vein + coronary sinus — the great cardiac vein parallels the LAD up the anterior interventricular sulcus, joining the coronary sinus on the back of the heart, which empties into the right atrium.
Color convention. Coronary arteries red; coronary veins blue. Both run in the same sulci, so place them side by side.
Time: ~25 minutes.
