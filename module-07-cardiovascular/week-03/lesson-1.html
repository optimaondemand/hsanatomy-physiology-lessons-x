🥽 YOUR VR SESSION THIS WEEK
What to trace. Per the cardiovascular design plan, trace the major arteries onto the figure. Build out from the aorta:
• Aorta — ascending, arch, descending (thoracic + abdominal).
• Head/neck — brachiocephalic trunk, both common carotids (internal + external), vertebral arteries.
• Upper limb (both sides) — subclavian → axillary → brachial → radial + ulnar.
• Abdomen — celiac trunk (with hepatic, gastric, splenic branches), superior mesenteric, both renal arteries, inferior mesenteric.
• Lower limb (both sides) — common iliac → external iliac → femoral → popliteal → anterior tibial + posterior tibial + fibular.
Color convention. All arteries traced in red. Use thicker lines for larger vessels.
Time: ~35 minutes (this is the largest tracing session of the module).
