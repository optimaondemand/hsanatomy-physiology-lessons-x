📖 READING FOCUS
Reading 7.11 — Blood Flow, Pressure, and Resistance
Before completing this assignment, read Reading 7.11 and complete the activities in Lesson 7.11, including the simulation.
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
Today you use the cardiovascular system’s core relationships — CO = HR × SV and Q = ΔP / R — to predict, not just calculate. Reasoning forward from a scenario to its physiological consequence is the skill a clinician actually uses at the bedside.
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
Submit your three prediction scenarios from Lesson 7.11: (a) valve failure, (b) increased cholesterol, (c) decreased blood pressure — each with a predicted direction of change and 1–2 sentences of reasoning.
2
Submit your simulation log — one sentence per experiment (3 experiments).
3
Write a 200-word response to this case: A 54-year-old patient’s blood pressure has been 145/95 mmHg for several years. His physician calls this “Stage 2 hypertension.” Use the equations from this reading to explain (a) what is keeping his MAP elevated — cardiac output, peripheral resistance, or both? (b) Name two specific organs that are at risk for damage and explain why. (c) Suggest one mechanism by which a common antihypertensive medication might work, in terms of the equations.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA

☐
Simulation log addresses all three experiments specifically.
☐
