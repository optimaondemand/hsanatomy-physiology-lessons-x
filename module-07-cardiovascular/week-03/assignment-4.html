📖 READING FOCUS
Reading 7.12 — Fetal Circulation
Before completing this assignment, read Reading 7.12 and complete the activities in Lesson 7.12.
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
Birth is the most dramatic physiological transition in human life. In a matter of seconds, the cardiovascular system reorganizes from a fetus’s placenta-fed scheme to an adult’s lung-fed scheme. This assignment asks you to make that transition visible.
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
Submit your fetal-circulation flow diagram (heart + shunts + placenta + trace of one drop from placenta to brain).
2
Submit your birth-transition cascade flowchart (first breath → all shunts closed) with the intermediate steps named.
3
Write a 200-word response to this case: A premature infant’s ductus arteriosus has not closed by Day 4 of life. The cardiologist orders indomethacin. Explain in your own words: (a) what is “patent ductus arteriosus”? (b) Why is it usually a problem? (c) Why does indomethacin work — what does it do at the level of the duct’s smooth muscle?
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
Fetal flow diagram shows all three shunts and umbilical vessels in correct positions.
☐
Birth-transition flowchart includes pulmonary resistance falling, left atrial pressure rising, and prostaglandin reduction.
☐
PDA case response names indomethacin’s mechanism (prostaglandin-synthesis inhibition → promotes ductus closure).
