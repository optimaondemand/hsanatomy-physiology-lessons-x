Reading 7.03 — Leukocytes and Platelets
The substantive content for this assignment lives on the paired Reading page.
Before completing this assignment, read Reading 7.03 and complete the activities in Lesson 7.03.
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
A physician reading a complete blood count looks at which leukocyte is elevated and uses the pattern to narrow down what is wrong. In this assignment you build the ID sheet a beginning physician would use, then reason about what a specific elevated count probably means.
🎯 CLINICAL READING QUESTION
A patient’s blood test shows lymphocytes elevated and neutrophils normal. A second patient’s test shows neutrophils elevated and lymphocytes normal. Without other information, which of the two would you suspect has a bacterial infection, and which would you suspect has a viral infection? Why?
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
Submit the 5-row, 4-column leukocyte ID sheet you built in Lesson 7.03. Columns: Leukocyte / % of WBCs / Distinguishing visual feature / Primary job.
2
Write a 150–200 word reflection that answers the clinical reading question above. Be specific about which cell does what, not just “the immune system reacts.”
3
Add one sentence at the end about platelets: where they come from and what activates them.
4
Submit as Canvas text entry, .docx, or .pdf.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
All five leukocytes present with accurate % range, visual feature, and job.
☐
Clinical reasoning correctly identifies elevated lymphocytes → viral and elevated neutrophils → bacterial, with a specific reason.
☐
Platelet sentence correctly identifies megakaryocytes as the source and exposed collagen as the activator.
