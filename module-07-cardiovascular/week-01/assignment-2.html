📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
The red blood cell is the textbook example of form following function. Almost every visible feature — its shape, its missing organelles, its membrane flexibility, its density of hemoglobin — is the answer to a single question: how do you carry as much oxygen as possible, as quickly as possible, in a tube narrower than yourself? In this assignment you build that argument explicitly.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
If you redesigned the red blood cell with a nucleus and full mitochondria, what would change — for the better and for the worse?
