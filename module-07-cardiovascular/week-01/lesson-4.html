🥽 YOUR VR SESSION THIS WEEK
No VR sculpting this lesson. Blood typing is a chemistry puzzle, not an anatomical structure. The right way to internalize it is to do the typing, in a guided simulation.
Substitute web interactive. Open the HHMI BioInteractive “Blood Types” click-and-learn (search “HHMI blood typing”) or the Nobel Prize educational game “Blood Typing” (educational.nobelprize.org — search “blood typing game”). Either tool walks you through actual typing decisions for simulated patients. The Nobel game is a particular favorite because it scores you on whether your patients live.
Time: ~20 minutes in the interactive.

4
Blood-type compatibility table
15 min
1
Now that you’ve seen the interactive score patients on both ABO and Rh, build the full picture: an 8×8 blood-type compatibility table. Rows = donor type (O−, O+, A−, A+, B−, B+, AB−, AB+), columns = recipient type, same eight. Mark each cell “safe” or “reacts.”
2
Note the row of all “safe” cells (O− donor) and the column of all “safe” cells (AB+ recipient). This is your visual confirmation of universal donor / recipient once Rh is factored in, not just ABO alone.
