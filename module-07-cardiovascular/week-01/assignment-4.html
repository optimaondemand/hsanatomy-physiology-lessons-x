📖 READING FOCUS
Reading 7.04 — Hemostasis and Blood Typing
The substantive content for this assignment lives on the paired Reading page.
Before completing this assignment, read Reading 7.04 and complete the activities in Lesson 7.04, including the blood-typing interactive.
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
This assignment asks you to do two things: (1) show your understanding of hemostasis with a clean flowchart and ABO compatibility table; (2) make five concrete transfusion decisions, each accompanied by one sentence of reasoning. A blood-bank technician making a real-life decision in a hospital answers exactly these kinds of questions, dozens of times a day.
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
Submit (a) your 3-step hemostasis flowchart and (b) your 8×8 ABO+Rh compatibility table from Lesson 7.04.
2
Answer each of the five transfusion-decision scenarios below. For each: write SAFE or REACTS, and add one sentence of reasoning.
Scenario A. Donor: O−. Recipient: A+.
Scenario B. Donor: B+. Recipient: O+.
Scenario C. Donor: AB−. Recipient: AB+.
Scenario D. Donor: A+. Recipient: B−.
Scenario E. Donor: O+. Recipient: O− (this is the donor’s first-ever transfusion to this recipient; the recipient is a 22-year-old woman who plans to have children).
3
Write a closing 3–4 sentence reflection on what surprised you about the blood-typing interactive you used. What does the system get right, and what did playing the game make easier to remember than reading?
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
Hemostasis flowchart correctly orders the 3 phases and names key molecules (collagen, ADP, fibrinogen → fibrin, prothrombin → thrombin).
☐
ABO+Rh compatibility table is correct in all 64 cells.
☐
All five scenarios answered with correct SAFE/REACTS and Rh implications noted where relevant (especially scenario E).
☐
Reflection identifies one specific insight from the interactive.
