✏️ YOUR DRAWING SESSION THIS WEEK
What to draw. A five-cell leukocyte lineup at exaggerated scale, alongside the RBC from Lesson 7.02 for size comparison. On one fresh page, draw each one in a row, with your RBC drawn beside them at the same scale:
• Neutrophil — rounded body, multi-lobed nucleus (3–5 lobes connected by thin strands).
• Lymphocyte — small, with one huge round nucleus and almost no cytoplasm.
• Monocyte — the largest, with a kidney-bean-shaped nucleus.
• Eosinophil — bilobed nucleus, pink-stained granules in cytoplasm.
• Basophil — dark-blue granules so dense the nucleus is hard to see.
Goal. Make each one visually distinct enough that a stranger could tell them apart. These five drawings stay with your Circulatory Map for the rest of the module — they will also reappear in Module 8 (Lymphatic/Immune).
Time: ~30 minutes.
