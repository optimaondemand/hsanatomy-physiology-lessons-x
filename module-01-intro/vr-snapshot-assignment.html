Your First Orientation in the Body
VR submission · 12 pts · Due end of Module 1 (Week 1)
Module thread — Wonder. Before you can learn any one system in detail, you need to know where you are standing. This snapshot is proof that you can navigate the body using the same vocabulary a surgeon or anatomist would use.
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
In Lesson 1.01 you walked the full figure in HoloAnatomy and named organ systems on sight. In Lesson 1.02 you passed the three body planes through the figure and located the four major body cavities. For this assignment, you return to ENGAGE one more time, frame a screenshot that demonstrates you can use this module’s spatial vocabulary correctly, and submit it with a short caption. There is nothing to build here — this is about seeing and naming, which is exactly what the rest of the year will build on.
🧸 WHAT YOUR SNAPSHOT MUST SHOW
Open ENGAGE and load HoloAnatomy. Choose one of the following two options for your snapshot:
Option A — A plane in action
▶
Position your view so one of the three body planes (sagittal, frontal, or transverse) is clearly visible passing through the figure. Frame the shot so a viewer could tell which plane it is without being told.
Option B — A cavity and its contents
▶
Position your view on one of the four major body cavities (cranial, thoracic, abdominal, or pelvic) and frame the shot so at least one organ system inside it is visible.
Either way, capture the IFX snapshot before closing your session.
✏️ REFLECTION CAPTION (60–80 words)
Submit a brief written caption with your IFX. Your caption must:
1. Name which option (A or B) you chose and what is visible in the shot.
2. Use at least two directional terms (superior, inferior, anterior, posterior, medial, lateral) to accurately describe the position of something in your shot relative to something else. (Example: “The heart sits medial to the lungs and slightly left of the midsagittal plane.”)
3. Name at least one organ system visible in your snapshot and one thing you did not expect to see or recognize before this week.
An audio caption is also acceptable in place of written, if your VR setup supports it.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
Snapshot clearly shows the chosen plane or cavity option.
☐
Caption uses at least two directional terms correctly.
☐
At least one organ system correctly named.
☐
Reflection identifies something genuinely unexpected, not a generic statement.
