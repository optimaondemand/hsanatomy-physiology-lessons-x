🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to describe anatomical position and use it as the reference for every directional term.
2
I will be able to use the six pairs of directional terms (superior/inferior, anterior/posterior, medial/lateral, proximal/distal, superficial/deep) accurately on the body.
3
I will be able to name the three body planes and the major body cavities, and locate each on a 3D figure.
4
I will be able to define homeostasis and give one example of a variable the body holds within a narrow range.

🥽 YOUR VR SESSION THIS WEEK
This week’s VR session is still orientation — no sculpting yet. The goal is to anchor the abstract terms you just read about (planes, cavities) to a real 3D figure.
What to do. Open ENGAGE and load HoloAnatomy. Stand in front of the male figure. Mentally pass each of the three body planes through the figure: a sagittal plane (left/right), a frontal plane (anterior/posterior), and a transverse plane (superior/inferior). Speak each direction out loud as you do it. Then point at each of the four major cavities — cranial, thoracic, abdominal, pelvic — and name them out loud.
Goal. Make “sagittal,” “frontal,” “transverse,” “cranial,” “thoracic,” “abdominal,” and “pelvic” physically meaningful to you, not just verbal.
Time: ~15 minutes.
