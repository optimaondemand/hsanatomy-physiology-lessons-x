Lesson focus: Anatomical Position, Planes, Regions, Cavities
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
Anatomy is unintelligible without a shared vocabulary for direction, plane, and cavity. In this assignment you will use the six directional pairs you read about (superior/inferior, anterior/posterior, medial/lateral, proximal/distal, superficial/deep) in a single connected description of two body parts. The point is not to memorize the terms in the abstract — it is to use them so they begin to feel natural.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
Why does anatomy require a fixed reference posture and a shared vocabulary for direction?
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
Re-read the “Directional terms” section of Reading 1.02 and review your stick-figure diagram.
2
Choose two body parts: one that is medial and superior, and one that is lateral and inferior. (Examples: the heart and the right knee. The trachea and the left elbow. The brain and the right hand. Pick anything specific.)
3
Write a paragraph (200–300 words) describing the spatial relationship of these two parts to each other and to the rest of the body. Use all six directional pairs at least once each: superior/inferior, anterior/posterior, medial/lateral, proximal/distal, superficial/deep. Underline or bold each directional term you use, so the grader can easily see them.
4
In a final 2–3 sentences, answer the focus question directly: why does anatomy need a fixed reference posture? Do not restate the question — answer it.
5
Submit as a Canvas text entry, or attach a .docx or .pdf.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
Names two specific body parts and uses them consistently throughout.
☐
Uses all six directional pairs (twelve directional terms) accurately at least once each.
☐
Highlights (bolds or underlines) each directional term used.
☐
Final 2–3 sentences answer the focus question directly.
📊 GRADING RUBRIC
Criteria Excellent (4) Proficient (3) Developing (2) Beginning (1) Pts
Use of directional terms
4 pts		 All six pairs used and used correctly. No misapplied terms. All six pairs used; one mild misapplication. 4–5 pairs used, or several misapplications. Fewer than 4 pairs, or terms used incorrectly. 4 pts
Anatomical accuracy
4 pts		 Two specific body parts named; relationships described accurately throughout. Two specific parts named; one minor inaccuracy. Vague body parts, or several inaccuracies. Body parts unspecific; description incoherent. 4 pts
Focus question response
4 pts		 Final paragraph engages the question with clear, original reasoning about why a shared frame matters. Final paragraph addresses the question; reasoning is clear if brief. Final paragraph restates rather than answers. Final paragraph missing or off-topic. 4 pts
Total: 12 pts
