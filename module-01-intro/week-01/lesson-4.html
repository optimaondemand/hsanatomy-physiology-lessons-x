▶ GO DO THIS — ENZYME SIMULATION (NO VR THIS LESSON)
There is no VR session this lesson. Instead, you will run an interactive simulation that lets you watch enzyme reactions speed up and slow down with changing conditions.
What to do. Open the BioMan Biology Enzymatic simulation (free, runs in any browser): visit https://biomanbio.com/HTML5GamesandLabs/LifeChemgames/enzymatichtml5page.html. Run the simulation and answer the on-site quiz questions to see the enzyme work under various conditions to find (1) optimal temperature, (2) optimal pH, and (3) ideal substrate concentrations. For each, observe how the rate of product formation changes. Sketch one rate-versus-time graph in your notebook for each condition and summarize lessons learned.
Goal. Make the four conditions affecting enzyme activity feel concrete — not numbers on a page but rates you watch happen.
Time: ~15 minutes.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to explain how an enzyme lowers the activation energy of a reaction by binding substrate at its active site.
2
I will be able to name and explain the four conditions that affect enzyme activity (temperature, pH, substrate concentration, enzyme concentration).
3
I will be able to distinguish competitive and noncompetitive inhibition and give one example of each.
