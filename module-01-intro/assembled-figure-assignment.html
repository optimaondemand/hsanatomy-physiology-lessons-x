Module 1 Assembled Figure Assignment
Your First Orientation in the Body
Figure submission · 12 pts · Due end of Module 1 (Week 1)
Module thread — Wonder. Before you can learn any one system in detail, you need to know where you are standing. Your figure is proof that you can navigate the body using the same vocabulary a surgeon or anatomist would use.
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
In Lesson 1.01 you drew an anatomical-position outline and placed the main organs of the eleven organ systems where you expected them. In Lesson 1.02 you added the three body planes, labeled the four major body cavities, and wrote directional annotations. For this assignment you tidy that single figure into its finished form and submit it with a short caption. It is about placing and naming, which is exactly what the rest of the year will build on.
🧸 WHAT YOUR FIGURE MUST SHOW
Paper is highly recommended: draw your figure by hand on one large page, then photograph or scan it flat in good light. A digital figure (a drawing in Microsoft 365 Word, PowerPoint, or OneNote, exported as an image or PDF) is also accepted. Either way, submit one clear file.
▶
A large outline of a person in anatomical position, front view.
▶
The main organs of each of the eleven organ systems, placed where you expect them and labeled (a “?” is fine for no more than two).
▶
The three body planes (sagittal, frontal, transverse), each in a different color or line style and labeled.
▶
The four major cavities (cranial, thoracic, abdominal, pelvic) outlined and labeled, with at least one organ named in each.
▶
At least four directional annotations using four different directional terms (for example, “the stomach is inferior to the heart”).
✏️ REFLECTION CAPTION (60–80 words)
Record yourself speaking a caption of about 60–80 words (roughly 30–45 seconds) with your phone, laptop, or the Canvas media recorder. If you genuinely cannot record, a written caption is accepted. Your caption must:
1. Name one structure or system you placed on your figure and explain why you put it where you did.
2. Use at least two directional terms (superior, inferior, anterior, posterior, medial, lateral) to accurately describe the position of something on your figure relative to something else. (Example: “The heart sits medial to the lungs and slightly left of the midsagittal plane.”)
3. Name at least one organ system on your figure and one thing you were unsure of or did not expect when you tried to place it.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
Figure shows the anatomical-position outline, the three planes, and the four cavities, all labeled.
☐
Figure carries four directional annotations and the caption uses at least two directional terms correctly.
☐
Main organs of all eleven organ systems placed and labeled (a “?” for no more than two).
☐
Caption identifies something genuinely unexpected or uncertain, not a generic statement.
