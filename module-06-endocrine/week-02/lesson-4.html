Module thread — Courtesy. Two weeks of glands, hormones, axes, and feedback loops — all of it in service of one quiet job: keeping the body steady. The system you have studied is the body’s manners. It does its work without being noticed until something breaks.
📖 TODAY’S FOCUS
Reading 6.08 — Synthesis: The Chemistry of Coordination
The substantive content for this lesson lives on the paired Reading page in Canvas.
Before you start the activities below, open Reading 6.08 in Canvas and read it end-to-end. This is the synthesis reading and the prep for your Assembled Figure.
✏️ YOUR DRAWING SESSION THIS WEEK — REASSEMBLY FOR THE ASSEMBLED FIGURE
What to do. This is the module’s reassembly session. Gather your Endocrine Map and every detail page from the past two weeks, lay them out together on a table or the floor (or on one slide, if you are working digitally), and confirm that all of the endocrine structures you drew are present and labeled:
Primary endocrine glands (Lessons 6.03–6.05): hypothalamus, infundibulum, anterior + posterior pituitary, thyroid, all four parathyroids, both adrenals (with at least one in cross-section showing the three cortical zones and the medulla).
Dual endocrine organs (Lesson 6.06): pancreas in position behind the stomach; islet detail page with alpha and beta cells.
Secondary endocrine markers (Lesson 6.07): heart, kidneys, stomach, small intestine, liver, gonads — each with at least one hormone-marker label.
Your body outline gives the spatial reference for every gland. Once everything is laid out, photograph (or export) your figure for the module assignment. Make sure all the labels are legible in the photograph.
Time: ~40 minutes (reassembly + photo).
