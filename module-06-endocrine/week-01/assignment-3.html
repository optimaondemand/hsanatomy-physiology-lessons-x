Lesson focus: How does the brain control the endocrine system?
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
The hypothalamus controls most of the endocrine system through a small set of three-floor regulatory axes: hypothalamus releases a small hormone that drives the pituitary, which drives a target gland, whose hormone then feeds back to suppress the higher floors. Once you can draw one of these axes from memory, you can predict how the system behaves under stress, disease, or medication. In this assignment, you will trace one axis end-to-end.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
If a doctor finds that a patient’s downstream hormone is too high, what could go wrong at each floor of the axis — and how would the readings of the upstream hormones tell them where the problem lies?
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
Choose one of the two axes you diagrammed yourself in Lesson 6.03's Activity 2: HPA (adrenal cortex / cortisol) or HPG (gonads). (The HPT axis was given to you as a worked example, so use one of your own two here.)
2
Redraw (or submit a clean copy of) that axis: hypothalamus on top, anterior pituitary in the middle, target gland on the bottom. Use labeled arrows for the hormones in both directions. Make sure the negative-feedback arrow from the bottom hormone back to the top two floors is unmistakable.
3
Below your diagram, write three short scenarios:
  • (A) Primary failure — the bottom gland fails to make its hormone. Predict what happens to the upstream hormones. Are they higher or lower than normal?
  • (B) Secondary failure — the pituitary fails (e.g., a tumor compresses it). Predict what happens to both the hypothalamic hormone and the target gland’s hormone.
  • (C) Tertiary failure — the hypothalamus fails (very rare). Predict the cascade.
4
In a final paragraph (3–5 sentences), engage the focus question: explain how a doctor would use blood tests of all three hormones in your axis to figure out which floor is broken.
5
Total length: a labeled diagram + 250–400 words. Submit as a Canvas text entry plus image upload, or as a .pdf with the diagram embedded.
