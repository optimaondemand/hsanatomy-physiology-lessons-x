🥽 YOUR VR SESSION THIS WEEK
What to do. Open ENGAGE with your figure (integument visible). Zoom in on the upper abdomen and find the adrenal gland sitting on top of one kidney. Toggle on the sympathetic chain ganglia from your Module 5 work. Look at the connection: a sympathetic preganglionic fiber heading straight into the adrenal medulla, no postganglionic relay. Compare this to a normal sympathetic synapse on a blood vessel (also visible nearby) where there is a postganglionic neuron.
Goal. See, in 3D, that the adrenal medulla is a sympathetic ganglion that became an endocrine gland. The cells inside are sympathetic neurons that lost their axons and dump their neurotransmitter directly into the blood instead.
Time: ~15 minutes.
