Reading 6.02 — Endocrine vs. Neural Control of Physiology
The substantive content for this assignment lives on the paired Reading page in Canvas.
Before completing this assignment, read Reading 6.02 and complete the Lesson 6.02 activities.
Lesson focus: How do the nervous and endocrine systems share the work of regulation?
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
The nervous and endocrine systems are not rivals — they are partners that share the work of running the body across very different time scales. In this assignment you will analyze one real-world scenario in which both systems are doing their part, and you will explain who is doing what, when, and why. This is the kind of integrated thinking you will need every week of this module.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
Why does the body need both fast and slow communication systems, and what would go wrong if it had only one?
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
Choose one of these three scenarios:
  • (A) Hearing a sudden loud crash behind you. Trace the first three minutes of your body’s response.
  • (B) Going on a 5K morning run after eating breakfast two hours earlier. Trace the first 30 minutes of the run.
  • (C) Walking outside on a winter day in a t-shirt by mistake; you have to stay outside for 10 minutes. Trace what your body does over those 10 minutes to defend its temperature.
2
Divide your response into three time windows: milliseconds-to-seconds, seconds-to-minutes, and minutes-to-hours. For each window, name what is happening, which system is responsible, and which specific organ or hormone is doing the work. Use at least one specific hormone name (e.g., epinephrine, cortisol, glucagon).
3
In a final paragraph (4–6 sentences), engage the focus question. Use your scenario as evidence. Be specific about what would go wrong if the body had only the nervous system or only the endocrine system.
4
Total length: 350–500 words. Submit as a Canvas text entry, .docx, or .pdf.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
One scenario chosen; the same scenario carried through all three time windows.
☐
Each time window names a specific system, organ, and hormone or transmitter.
☐
At least one specific hormone named accurately.
☐
Focus paragraph names a concrete failure mode if the body lacked one of the two systems.
