Welcome to Anatomy & Physiology

Anatomy is what the body is made of. Physiology is how it works. This semester you will build the body system by system, from the chemistry of a single cell to the heart and the vessels it feeds. This page shows the route and how every week works, so nothing surprises you.

The route · Semester 1

Week 1Module 1 — Introduction to A&P · WonderLevels of organization, anatomical position and planes, the chemistry of life, enzymes
Week 2Module 2 — Tissues & the Integumentary System · WonderThe four tissue types and the layers of the skin
Weeks 3–5Module 3 — Skeletal System · PerseveranceBone tissue, all 206 bones region by region, and the joints that move them
Weeks 6–8Module 4 — Muscular System · Self-governmentThree kinds of muscle, how a muscle contracts, and the major muscles of the body
Weeks 9–11Module 5 — Nervous System · CourageNeurons and synapses, reflexes, the brain and spinal cord, nerves and the special senses
Weeks 12–13Module 6 — Endocrine System · CourtesyHormones and the glands that make them, and how they keep the body in balance
Weeks 14–16Module 7 — Cardiovascular System · ServiceBlood, the heart inside and out, and the major arteries and veins
Week 17Finals WeekReview the whole semester, then take the Semester 1 Final Exam

Every week and every module

4 lessons a week

Each week has four lessons, then a Reading Quiz on those four readings, such as Week 3 Reading Quiz (3.01–3.04).

Module Projects

Each module ends with two projects under its Module Projects header:

Writing · an analytical essay that connects form to function.
Assembled Figure · you draw the system piece by piece in your lessons, then assemble the pages into one figure, photograph it and add a caption.

Finals Week has three items: the Review Lesson (a tour of all seven modules), the Finals Review Chart (a six-system comparison you fill in) and the Semester 1 Final Exam.

Pacing and help

All the work for a quarter opens at its start and closes at its end. The due dates keep you on track; there is no penalty for missing one, but anything not done by the end of the quarter earns a zero, so keep a steady pace. Your teacher grades your work, gives feedback, and hosts Study Hall on Mondays, 2:00–4:00 PM ET.

What you need

Paper and pencilsA notebook for lesson work and large blank pages for your Assembled Figures. Paper is best; a digital drawing app also works.
A computerChrome or Edge for Canvas and the Canvas 3D rooms, and a phone or camera to photograph your drawings.
