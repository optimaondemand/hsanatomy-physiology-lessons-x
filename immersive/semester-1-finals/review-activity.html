The Assignment

Fill in the six-row comparative chart, one row per Semester 1 organ system. Then write a short reflection paragraph at the end. The chart is designed to make you say in your words what each system does, what tissue it is made of, where it meets another system, and which Optima virtue it has illustrated this semester.

Most cells need a phrase or one short sentence, not a paragraph. The reflection paragraph at the end is where you can stretch. Every cell can be answered from the S1 Review Lesson; read it end to end before you start.

How to Answer Each Column

Main function

One short phrase that captures what this system primarily does for the organism. (Example for the lymphatic system, if you had to do it: “returns interstitial fluid to the blood and houses the immune cells.”)

Key tissue type(s)

Name the tissue type(s) from Module 2 that this system is mostly made of. For most systems you will name one or two of the four (epithelial, connective, muscle, nervous). Some systems, especially the integument, use almost all four.

Cross-connection

Name one other Semester 1 system this one cannot function without, and say in one or two sentences how they interact. Do not repeat the same pairing in two different rows: use a different connection for each system.

Virtue

The module’s virtue thread is fair game (Wonder, Perseverance, Self-government, Courage, Courtesy, Service). You may pick a different virtue from Optima’s seven (courage, courtesy, honesty, perseverance, self-government, service, responsibility) if you can defend it in your reflection.

Three Prompts, One Paragraph

  1. A
    Pick one cross-connection from your chart and explain why it is more interesting than the others: what would happen if it failed?
  2. B
    Pick one virtue you assigned and defend it in one sentence. Why does this system, specifically, teach that virtue?
  3. C
    Finish with one sentence on the claim from the Review Lesson: the body is best understood as one organism, not six systems. Agree or disagree, briefly.
