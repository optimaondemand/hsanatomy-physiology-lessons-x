Moving around: W A S D to walk, arrow keys or drag to look, R and F to go up and down, double-click the floor to jump there. Click a piece to pick it up; click it again to let go. On a touch screen, use the on-screen stick. The ☰ menu has Save a screenshot.

B

In Room 4.0920 minutes

  • Start the 3D room above. You begin in the corridor right outside Room 4.09: walk in through the door in front of you.
  • Read the sign on the left wall.
  • The pelvis and right leg stand in the middle of the room. Seven muscles and muscle groups wait on the benches.
  • Click a piece to pick it up, walk over, hold it where it belongs and click it again to let go. A muscle clicks in only when it is close to its true place. Several belong on the back: walk around.
  • Then name them. Labels are on the table by the door. Carry each one and hold it right on the muscle it names.
  • Click CHECK ANSWERS at the top. A red X means try again: click that item for a hint. Save one screenshot from the front and one from the back. Then watch the quadriceps work at the pedestal.
  • At the pinboard beside the door, press N to add three sticky notes that answer the prompts printed on the board.
