📖 READING FOCUS
Reading 3.07 — The Shoulder Girdle and the Upper Arm
The substantive content for this assignment lives on the paired Reading page in Canvas.
Before completing this assignment, read Reading 3.07 and complete the activities in Lesson 3.07.
Lesson focus: The shoulder is the most mobile joint in the body — and the most often dislocated.
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
In this assignment you compare the shoulder joint and the hip joint as a paired form-follows-function case. The shoulder maximizes mobility; the hip maximizes stability. The bony architecture of each tells you why.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
Why is the shoulder more mobile and less stable than the hip, and what specific bony feature is responsible?
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
Shoulder joint sketch. Submit your labeled shoulder-joint sketch from Lesson 3.07: head of humerus, glenoid cavity, acromion, clavicle, AC joint.
2
Range-of-motion observations. Summarize your shoulder vs. hip experiment in 3–5 sentences. Which joint moves more freely, and in roughly how many distinct directions?
3
Focus-question answer (4–6 sentences). Answer the focus question. Name the specific bony feature of the shoulder that allows more mobility, and explain the trade-off in dislocation risk.
4
Total length: 250–400 words plus the sketch.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
Shoulder-joint sketch labels all 5 specified structures correctly.
☐
Experiment summary is specific about what the student observed.
☐
Focus-question response names the shallow glenoid cavity (or its depth as compared to the acetabulum) and ties it to dislocation risk.
