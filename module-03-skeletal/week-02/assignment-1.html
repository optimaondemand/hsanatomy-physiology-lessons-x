📖 READING FOCUS
Reading 3.05 — The Lower Spine: Lumbar, Sacrum, Coccyx, and Intervertebral Discs
The substantive content for this assignment lives on the paired Reading page in Canvas.
Before completing this assignment, read Reading 3.05 and complete the activities in Lesson 3.05, including the drawing session and the disc cross-section diagram.
Lesson focus: How the lower spine bears load and how intervertebral discs share it.
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
In this assignment you will explain how the lower spine handles the load it has to bear — from the broad lumbar vertebral bodies, through the sacrum’s wedged transfer to the pelvis, with discs absorbing shock along the way. You will also explain what happens when a disc fails.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
Why do most disc herniations happen in the lower lumbar spine (L4–L5 or L5–S1) rather than higher up?
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
Disc cross-section diagram. Submit a photo or scan of your two-panel sketch from Lesson 3.05 (healthy disc + herniated disc), with annulus fibrosus and nucleus pulposus labeled.
2
Load-path short answer (3–5 sentences). Trace the path that body weight takes when you are standing — from your skull, through the spine, into the sacrum, and into the pelvis/legs. Name at least three specific structures along the way.
3
Focus-question answer (3–5 sentences). Why do most disc herniations happen at L4–L5 or L5–S1? Use the load-path you just described.
4
Total length: 250–400 words. Submit text + image as Canvas text entry, or as a .docx/.pdf.
