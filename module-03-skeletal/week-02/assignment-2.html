📖 READING FOCUS
Reading 3.06 — The Thorax: Sternum, Ribs, and Costal Cartilages
The substantive content for this assignment lives on the paired Reading page in Canvas.
Before completing this assignment, read Reading 3.06 and complete the activities in Lesson 3.06.
Lesson focus: The rib cage as a structure that must be both protective and flexible.
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
In this assignment you classify all 12 rib pairs and then run a thought experiment: what would change if the costal cartilages were replaced with bone? This is the most direct form-follows-function exercise yet — the answer is breathing.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
What would happen to a person whose costal cartilages had ossified completely into bone?
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
Rib chart. Submit your 12-row rib chart from Lesson 3.06. Each row should show rib number, category (true / false / floating), and where the costal cartilage attaches.
2
Sternum landmark short answer (3–5 sentences). Why is the sternal angle a useful clinical landmark? What rib does it align with? Name one situation in which finding the sternal angle would matter.
3
Focus-question response (4–6 sentences). Answer the focus question. Address (a) what the cartilage is doing in a healthy rib cage, (b) what would happen during inhalation, and (c) what the long-term consequence would be.
4
Total length: 250–400 words. Submit table + responses as Canvas text entry, or as .docx/.pdf.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA

☐

☐
