📖 READING FOCUS
Reading 3.09 — The Pelvic Girdle and the Femur
The substantive content for this assignment lives on the paired Reading page in Canvas.
Before completing this assignment, read Reading 3.09 and complete the activities in Lesson 3.09.
Lesson focus: The pelvis and femur prioritize stability and load-bearing over reach.
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
In this assignment you complete the hip-vs-shoulder comparison and explain why a hip fracture in an elderly patient is a much more serious injury than a clavicle fracture in a young athlete — even though both involve broken bones.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
Why is a hip fracture in an elderly patient often life-threatening, while a clavicle fracture in a young athlete is usually a minor setback?
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
Hip vs. shoulder comparison table. Submit the two-column comparison table from Lesson 3.09. Rows must include: socket depth, mobility, stability, typical injury pattern.
2
Femoral neck short answer (3–5 sentences). Identify where on the femur the femoral neck is. Why is the femoral neck a common fracture site in adults with osteoporosis?
3
Focus-question answer (5–7 sentences). Engage the focus question. Consider (a) the femur’s role in basic mobility, (b) the consequences of being unable to walk for weeks of recovery in an older adult, (c) why the clavicle, by contrast, can often heal with rest while normal activity continues.
4
Total length: 300–450 words plus the table.
