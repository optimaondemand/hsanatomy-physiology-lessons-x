✏️ YOUR DRAWING SESSION THIS WEEK — MODULE-END REASSEMBLY
This is the culminating drawing session of the module. You will combine every Skeleton Atlas page you have drawn across three weeks into one complete skeleton, and photograph or export the figure you submit as the M3 Assembled Figure Assignment.
What to do.
▶ Gather every Skeleton Atlas page from Weeks 1–3 and a large flat surface (a floor or table works well), or a blank digital slide.
▶ Lay out your drawn pages from Weeks 1–3 in the shape of a complete skeleton — skull at the top, spine down the middle, then the thorax, shoulder girdle and arms, and pelvic girdle and legs — and tape them together (paper) or place them on one slide (digital). It must include: skull (cranial + facial + hyoid + dentition); vertebral column with intervertebral discs; thorax (sternum + ribs + costal cartilages); shoulder girdle + upper limbs (clavicle, scapula, humerus, radius, ulna, carpals, metacarpals, phalanges); pelvic girdle + lower limbs (os coxa, femur, patella, tibia, fibula, tarsals, metatarsals, phalanges); detailed synovial joint from Lesson 3.11.
▶ Step back and look at the assembled figure as a whole, front to back and top to bottom. Check that the bone counts are right (7+12+5 vertebrae; 12 pairs of ribs; 8 carpals per wrist; 7 tarsals per ankle).
▶ Photograph the assembled figure flat and in good light (or export the slide as an image or PDF). This is what you submit to the M3 Assembled Figure Assignment.
Time: ~35 minutes (or longer if you want to polish details).
