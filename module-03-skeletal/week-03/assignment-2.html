📖 READING FOCUS
Reading 3.10 — The Lower Leg, Ankle, and Foot
The substantive content for this assignment lives on the paired Reading page in Canvas.
Before completing this assignment, read Reading 3.10 and complete the activities in Lesson 3.10.
Lesson focus: The foot is built like a tripod with arches — engineered to absorb shock and push off.
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
In this assignment you walk through one step in slow motion, analyze which bones are doing which work, and explain the role of the foot’s arches in walking comfortably.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
Why does the foot need arches at all? What would walking feel like without them?
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
Footprint sketch. Submit your labeled footprint sketch from Lesson 3.10: tibia (cut off), fibula (cut off), at least 2 named tarsals (talus, calcaneus), 5 metatarsals, 14 phalanges, medial longitudinal arch.
2
One-step analysis (4–6 sentences). Submit the three-sentence walking observation from Lesson 3.10, plus 1–3 additional sentences describing how the medial malleolus, the talus, and the metatarsals each contribute to a single step.
3
Focus-question answer (3–5 sentences). Why does the foot need arches? Describe at least one specific way the arches act as a spring and one specific thing that goes wrong when the arches collapse (flat feet).
4
Total length: 300–450 words plus the sketch.

☐
