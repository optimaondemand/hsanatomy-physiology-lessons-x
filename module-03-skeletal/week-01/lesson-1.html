1
I will be able to describe bone as living tissue and name the five major functions of the skeletal system.
2

🥽 YOUR VR SESSION THIS WEEK
This is your first sculpting session of the course. You will use the 3D pen tool to trace one example of each bone shape. The four pieces you build today are the start of the skeleton you will reassemble in Week 3 for your module snapshot. Save your work between sessions.
What to do. Open ENGAGE and load the standard HoloAnatomy environment. The figure begins with both male and female forms side by side. Take a minute to observe the (relatively minor) differences in bony structure. After this introduction the course defaults to the male figure for all subsequent sessions in Modules 3–11. Then sculpt one example of each shape, in this order:
▶ Long bone — trace the femur. Note the narrow shaft and the expanded heads at each end.
▶ Short bone — trace any one of the carpal bones (capitate is easiest to find). Notice how it is about as wide as it is tall.
▶ Flat bone — trace the sternum. Feel how thin and broad it is compared to the femur.
▶ Irregular bone — trace L1 (the first lumbar vertebra). Notice the central body, the hole for the spinal cord, and the several projections sticking out.
Before you leave the environment, find the medial epicondyle on either humerus — the bony bump on the inside of the elbow. Place a label or marker on it. This is the first bone marking you will name by sight, and it will return repeatedly when we add muscles in Module 4.
Time: ~25 minutes.
