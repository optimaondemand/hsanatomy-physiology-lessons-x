Lesson 3.02
The Skull — Cranial Bones
Lesson
Module thread — Perseverance. The skull surrounds the brain not as a single dome but as eight bones fitted together like stone in an arch — each one carrying part of the load so no one bone has to.

Reading 3.02 — The Skull: Cranial Bones

🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to distinguish the axial from the appendicular skeleton and state the bone count of each.
2
I will be able to name all eight cranial bones and locate each one on a 3D figure or sketch.
3
I will be able to describe the role of cranial sutures and the foramen magnum in the structure-and-function of the skull.
4
I will be able to explain why air-filled paranasal sinuses are useful given how heavy a solid skull would be.

🥽 YOUR VR SESSION THIS WEEK
This week you trace all eight cranial bones. Six are visible on the surface of the skull. Two are buried inside and need to be revealed by transparency.
What to do.
▶ Open ENGAGE and load HoloAnatomy (male figure).
▶ Trace the six superficial cranial bones one at a time, using a different color for each: frontal (blue), left and right parietal (white), left and right temporal (pink/magenta), occipital (green). Place a label on each as you finish.
▶ Make the six superficial bones transparent. Now trace the sphenoid (pink/magenta) and ethmoid (yellow), the two deep cranial bones. Place a label on each.
▶ Locate (do not need to trace) the foramen magnum — the large opening on the underside of the occipital bone where the spinal cord exits.
Save your work. The cranial bones you traced today join the femur, carpal, sternum, and L1 from Lesson 3.01 as the start of your module-snapshot skeleton.
Time: ~25 minutes.
3
VR cranial bone tracing
25 min
1
Follow the directions in the “Your VR Session This Week” callout above. Trace the six superficial cranial bones, then reveal and trace the two deep ones.
2
Locate the foramen magnum. Without leaving VR, mentally trace the path the spinal cord would take leaving the brain and entering the spine.
4
Sketch and label
15 min
1
In your notebook, draw a side view of a skull. Label the frontal, parietal, temporal, and occipital bones. Indicate the approximate location of the sphenoid and ethmoid with a dashed outline showing they are deep.
2
Mark the location of the styloid process and the mastoid process with arrows and labels. (The foramen magnum sits on the underside of the skull and is not visible from this side view — the styloid process is a better lateral-view landmark for this sketch.)
