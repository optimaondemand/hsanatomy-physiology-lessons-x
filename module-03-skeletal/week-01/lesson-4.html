🥽 YOUR VR SESSION THIS WEEK
This week you trace the upper two-thirds of the spine plus the costal cartilages that link thoracic vertebrae to the ribs.
What to do.
▶ Open ENGAGE and load HoloAnatomy (male figure).
▶ Trace the 7 cervical vertebrae (yellow). Spend a moment on C1 (atlas) and C2 (axis) — observe the ring shape of C1 and the upward-pointing dens of C2. Place a label on each.
▶ Trace the 12 thoracic vertebrae (yellow). Note how the bodies get larger as you move down.
▶ Trace all costal cartilages (blue) attaching each rib pair to the sternum. Place one label near the cartilage of the 5th rib.
▶ Place an intervertebral disc indicator between C1–C2 (label: “no disc here”) and between L1–L2 lower down (label: “shock absorber”) — C1 and C2 are the only pair of vertebrae without an intervertebral disc between them.
Time: ~28 minutes.

4
Head-motion experiment
10 min
1
Nod “yes” slowly. The joint doing most of the work is between the skull and C1 (atlanto-occipital joint). Write one sentence describing what kind of motion this joint allows.
2
Now shake your head “no.” The joint doing most of the work is between C1 and C2 (atlantoaxial joint), where the dens of C2 acts as a pivot. Write one sentence describing this motion.
