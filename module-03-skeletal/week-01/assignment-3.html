📖 READING FOCUS
Reading 3.03 — Facial Bones, the Hyoid, and Dentition
The substantive content for this assignment lives on the paired Reading page in Canvas.
Before completing this assignment, read Reading 3.03 and complete the activities in Lesson 3.03, including the VR tracing session.
Lesson focus: Fourteen facial bones, the floating hyoid, and the 32-tooth adult dentition — the bones of expression, communication, and eating.
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
This is a short quick-check assignment to lock in the facial bones, the unique hyoid, and the adult dentition pattern. Two short written responses plus the dentition table from your notebook.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
What can the hyoid bone do, despite never touching another bone, that no other bone in the body can do?
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
Facial bones short answer (3–5 sentences). Choose either the mandible or the maxilla. Describe (a) where it sits, (b) two specific functional jobs it performs, and (c) one consequence if it were missing or broken.
2
Hyoid focus question (3–5 sentences). Answer the focus question. What does the hyoid bone’s suspended position let it do? Name at least one specific function that depends on the hyoid not being locked to a neighboring bone.
3
Dentition table. Submit a photo or copy of the upper-jaw dentition layout from your notebook, with one quadrant labeled (2 incisors, 1 canine, 2 premolars, 3 molars) and each type paired with its function word (cut, pierce, crush, grind).
4
Total written length: 150–300 words. Submit as Canvas text entry plus the dentition photo, or a single .docx/.pdf with the photo embedded.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
Facial bone response correctly names location, two functions, and a consequence.
☐
Hyoid response identifies at least one function that depends on its non-articulating position.
☐
Dentition diagram correctly shows 8 teeth in one quadrant with all four types labeled.
☐
Each tooth type paired with its function word (cut, pierce, crush, grind).
📊 GRADING RUBRIC
Criteria Excellent (4) Proficient (3) Developing (2) Beginning (1) Pts
Facial bone response
4 pts		 Location, 2 specific functions, and 1 consequence all clearly stated. 3 of 4 elements; one element vague. 2 of 4 elements present. 1 element or off-topic. 4 pts
Hyoid response
4 pts		 Names a specific function (swallowing, speech) and ties it to the non-articulating position. Function named but link to position vague. Function mentioned but not specific. Response off-topic or missing. 4 pts
Dentition diagram
4 pts		 All 8 teeth in one quadrant labeled correctly with all 4 types and function words. 7–8 teeth labeled; one type or function word missing. 5–6 teeth labeled correctly. Fewer than 5 teeth or diagram missing. 4 pts
Total: 12 pts
