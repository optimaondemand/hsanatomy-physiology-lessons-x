📖 READING FOCUS
Reading 3.02 — The Skull: Cranial Bones
The substantive content for this assignment lives on the paired Reading page in Canvas.
Before completing this assignment, read Reading 3.02 and complete the activities in Lesson 3.02, including the VR tracing session.
Lesson focus: Eight cranial bones — six superficial, two deep — that surround and protect the brain.
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
In this assignment you will demonstrate that you can locate the eight cranial bones on a labeled diagram and explain how the structure of the cranial vault — sutures, foramina, paranasal sinuses — makes it well-suited to its function. This is a memorization-and-understanding task: you should walk away from it able to name each bone by sight and explain why the skull is built from eight pieces rather than one.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
Why is the skull built from eight separate bones held together by sutures, rather than being a single solid dome?
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
Labeled diagram. Sketch a side view of the skull in your notebook (or use a printable skull outline from a free anatomy reference). Label all eight cranial bones. Indicate which two are deep by using a dashed outline or a different label color. Also mark and label the styloid process (temporal bone) and at least one suture. (The foramen magnum sits on the underside of the skull and cannot be shown accurately on a side-view sketch.)
2
Short answer (3–5 sentences). The reading explains that paranasal sinuses make the skull lighter while preserving strength. Pick one specific paranasal sinus (frontal, ethmoidal, sphenoidal, or maxillary), state which bone it occupies, and explain one functional reason it is useful that this bone is hollow rather than solid.
3
Engage the focus question (3–5 sentences). Why is the cranial vault made of eight separate bones? Give at least two reasons drawn from the reading — consider development, fracture mechanics, and the role of sutures in shock absorption.
4
Total written length: 200–350 words. Submit your labeled diagram as a photo or scan, and the two short responses as Canvas text entry or as a .docx/.pdf attachment.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
Diagram labels all eight cranial bones correctly, with sphenoid and ethmoid marked as deep.
☐
Foramen magnum and at least one named suture marked on the diagram.
☐
Sinus question pairs a specific sinus with the correct bone and a clear functional reason.
☐
Focus-question response gives at least two reasons grounded in the reading.
📊 GRADING RUBRIC
Criteria Excellent (4) Proficient (3) Developing (2) Beginning (1) Pts
Diagram accuracy
4 pts		 All 8 cranial bones labeled and correctly placed; deep bones noted; styloid process and suture marked. 7 or 8 bones correctly labeled; minor placement error. 5–6 bones correctly labeled. Fewer than 5 bones labeled correctly. 4 pts
Sinus short answer
4 pts		 Specific sinus paired with correct bone and a clear functional reason (e.g., weight reduction, voice resonance). Sinus and bone correct; reasoning sound but brief. Sinus or bone correct, but reasoning vague. Sinus or bone misidentified; no reasoning. 4 pts
Focus-question reasoning
4 pts		 Two or more strong, distinct reasons drawn from the reading. Two reasons; one is fully developed, one less so. One reason or a restatement of the question. Response missing or off-topic. 4 pts
