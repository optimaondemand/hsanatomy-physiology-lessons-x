The Assembled Integument
VR submission · 18 pts · Due end of Module 2 (Week 1)
Module thread — Wonder. You built one organ across four days, one layer at a time. Your snapshot is that organ, finished, alongside the two cell shapes you sculpted to understand the tissues that make it move and feel.
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
Across Lessons 2.01–2.04 you built one continuous model in ENGAGE: a cross-section of skin, off-body, that grew a little more complete each day — three unlabeled layers, then labeled strata and cell types, then accessory structures. Alongside it, in Lesson 2.02, you sculpted a separate pair of off-body cells (a skeletal muscle fiber and a neuron) to understand the other two tissue types. For this assignment you bring your finished cross-section and your two off-body cells into a single view, capture the IFX snapshot, and submit it with a short reflection caption.
🧸 EXPECTED STRUCTURES IN YOUR SNAPSHOT
Your snapshot must show the finished integument cross-section plus the two off-body cell sculpts. Specifically:
The integument cross-section
▶
Three layers in correct order and proportion: epidermis (top), dermis (middle), hypodermis (bottom).
▶
Five labeled epidermal strata (basale, spinosum, granulosum, lucidum, corneum) and the three epidermal cell types marked (keratinocytes, melanocytes, dendritic cells).
▶
Papillary and reticular layers labeled within the dermis.
▶
All three accessory structures: a hair follicle (with visible hair strand), a sebaceous gland, and a sweat gland.
Off-body required detail
▶
One skeletal muscle fiber at exaggerated scale, with visible striations and multiple nuclei.
▶
One neuron with a cell body, dendrites, and an axon (myelin segments optional).
✏️ REFLECTION CAPTION (60–90 words)
Submit a brief written caption with your IFX. Your caption should:
1. Name what you built (cross-section + the two off-body cells).
2. Identify one substantive thing you learned about tissues from building it — not just what it looks like. (Example: “Sculpting the hair follicle made it obvious it isn’t just an epidermis structure — the follicle itself reaches down into the dermis, which is why hair loss can be a dermis problem, not just a skin-surface one.”)
An audio caption is also acceptable in place of written, if your VR setup supports it.
