🥽 YOUR VR SESSION THIS WEEK
Today you finish the integument cross-section you started in Lesson 2.01 and relabeled in Lesson 2.03. The point is to add the accessory structures so the model includes everything that lives inside skin.
What to do. Open ENGAGE. Reload your labeled cross-section. Add the following accessory structures using the 3D pen tool:
(1) A hair follicle. Draw it as a downward-extending tube originating in the epidermis and reaching into the dermis. Label the matrix at its base and the lunula-like translucent region as a comparison point. Show a strand of hair extending up out of the epidermis.
(2) A sebaceous gland. Draw it as a small grape-like cluster connected by a duct to the side of the hair follicle.
(3) A sweat gland. Draw it as a coiled tube in the dermis with a long duct extending up through the epidermis to the skin surface.
Goal. Have a single 3D model of skin that includes layers, strata, cell types, and accessory structures. This model is the most complete picture you will have of an organ system at the end of Module 2.
Time: ~15 minutes.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to name the major accessory structures of skin (hair follicle, nail, sweat gland, sebaceous gland) and describe what each does.
2
I will be able to classify a burn by which layers it damages and explain why that determines how it heals.
3
I will be able to explain how skin cancer connects to the epidermal cell types from Lesson 2.03, and name the body defenses that normally prevent uncontrolled cell division.
