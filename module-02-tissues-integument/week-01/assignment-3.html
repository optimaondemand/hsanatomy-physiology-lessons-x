Lesson focus: Skin’s functions and its three layers
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
This is a shorter, first-half checkpoint on the integument — today’s content only. Tomorrow’s Lesson 2.04 assignment asks you to trace a full function through the whole organ, including accessory structures you haven’t read about yet. Today, focus on layers.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
Why does the epidermis need five distinct strata instead of just one layer of skin cells?
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
In a paragraph (4–5 sentences), name the three layers of skin in order (epidermis, dermis, hypodermis), the dominant tissue type in each, and explain why the hypodermis is technically a support layer rather than part of the cutaneous membrane.
2
In a longer paragraph (5–6 sentences), trace a keratinocyte’s journey from the stratum basale to the stratum corneum. Name all five strata in order and explain what changes about the cell along the way (division, keratin, flattening, death).
3
Choose two of skin’s functions from today’s reading (barrier, thermoregulation, sensation, vitamin D, immune defense) and, in 2–3 sentences each, connect each function to the layer or cell type most responsible for it.
4
In a final 2–3 sentence answer, engage the focus question. Do not restate the question.
5
Total length: 250–350 words. Submit as Canvas text entry, or attach a .docx or .pdf.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
Names the three layers in order with the correct dominant tissue type in each.
☐
Traces the keratinocyte journey through all five strata in correct order.
☐
Connects two functions to the correct layer or cell type.
☐
Final answer engages the focus question directly.
