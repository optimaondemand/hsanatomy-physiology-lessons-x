Module thread — Wonder. The body is ordered, beautiful, and not yet fully understood. Carry that posture into every reading and every VR session this module.
The integumentary system now spans two lesson days. Today covers functions and layers. Tomorrow (Lesson 2.04) covers accessory structures, burns, and skin cancer.

🥽 YOUR VR SESSION THIS WEEK
Today you revisit the cross-section you sketched in Lesson 2.01, this time labeling it in more anatomical detail before you add accessory structures tomorrow.
What to do. Open ENGAGE and reload your three-layer cross-section from Lesson 2.01. Add labels for the five epidermal strata (basale, spinosum, granulosum, lucidum, corneum) on the epidermis layer, and mark where each of the three epidermal cell types — keratinocytes, melanocytes, dendritic cells — would sit. Then label the papillary and reticular layers within the dermis.
Goal. Move from a three-box diagram to a labeled cross-section that could stand in for a histology slide. Tomorrow you add the structures that live inside these layers.
Time: ~15 minutes.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to name at least four functions skin performs and explain how its structure makes each possible.
2
I will be able to name the three layers of skin, the five strata of the epidermis, and the three epidermal cell types.
3
I will be able to explain why the hypodermis is not technically part of the cutaneous membrane, even though it works closely with the skin above it.
