Module 5 Assembled Figure Assignment
The Assembled Nervous System
Figure submission · 25 pts · Due end of Module 5 (Week 3)
Module thread — Courage. You built it cell by cell, division by division, nerve by nerve. The body that decides — the part of you that thinks, senses, remembers, and acts — is on this figure.
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
During this module you drew the nervous system piece by piece: a Nervous System Map on a body outline (spinal cord, spinal nerves, autonomic wiring), a brain page, a cranial-nerve page, and detail pages for a neuron, a synapse, a spinal cord cross-section, the eye, and the ear. For this assignment, you gather all of your work into a single complete nervous-system figure, photograph (or export) it, and submit it along with progress images and a brief reflection caption. This is your record of having built the nervous system across three weeks — not a perfect textbook diagram, but the figure you made. Paper is highly recommended (draw by hand, lay the pages out together, photograph in good light); a digital drawing assembled on one slide is also accepted.
🧸 EXPECTED STRUCTURES IN YOUR FIGURE
Your figure must show your Nervous System Map and its detail pages. Specifically:
Brain — brain page
▶
Four major divisions visible and labeled: brainstem (with all three subregions — medulla, pons, midbrain), cerebellum, diencephalon, cerebrum.
▶
Four lobes labeled: frontal, parietal, temporal, occipital. Broca’s area marked.
▶
Ventricular system shown (dashed outlines) with all four ventricles marked.
▶
Meninges — three layers (dura, arachnoid, pia) shown around the brain.
Spinal cord — Nervous System Map
▶
Cord drawn running through the vertebral column from medulla to L1–L2.
Cranial nerves — cranial-nerve page
▶
All 12 cranial nerves labeled at their brainstem origins.
Spinal nerves — Nervous System Map
▶
Representative spinal nerves shown at cervical, thoracic, lumbar, and sacral regions; phrenic nerve highlighted at cervical plexus.
Autonomic wiring — Nervous System Map
▶
Sympathetic chain on one side of the spinal column; vagus nerve drawn from brainstem to abdominal organs.
Sense organs — detail pages
▶
Eye drawn in cut-away, with cornea, lens, retina, and optic nerve labeled.
▶
Ear drawn at exaggerated scale, with outer, middle, and inner regions shown.
Cellular detail pages (required — at least one)
▶
A synapse from Lesson 5.03 (with presynaptic terminal, vesicles, cleft, postsynaptic membrane, receptors labeled) and/or a spinal cord cross-section from Lesson 5.07 (with gray matter, white matter, dorsal/ventral horns, roots, dorsal root ganglion labeled). At least one must be present; including both is encouraged.
✏️ REFLECTION CAPTION (50–100 words)
Record yourself speaking a caption of 50–100 words (about 30–50 seconds) with your phone, laptop, or the Canvas media recorder; a written caption is accepted only if you genuinely cannot record. Your caption should:
1. Name what you drew (broad regions + your detail page).
2. Identify one substantive thing you learned about how the nervous system works — not just what it looks like. (Example: “Watching the corticospinal tract cross at the medulla, I finally understood why a stroke on one side of the brain shows up on the opposite side of the body. Every time I see the word ‘contralateral’ from now on, I will know what it really means.”)
Attach three dated progress images — one page from each week (for example, from Lessons 5.02, 5.06, and 5.09).
