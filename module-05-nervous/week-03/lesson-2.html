✏️ YOUR DRAWING SESSION THIS WEEK
This session draws one eye at exaggerated scale on its own detail page. The eye is a required detail page in your module-end Assembled Figure.
▶ Start with a large circle for the eyeball, drawn as a cut-away (a cross-section through the middle, front of the eye to one side) so you can see inside.
▶ Outer layer (fibrous tunic): draw the sclera (the white wall) covering most of the back two-thirds. Draw the transparent cornea as a small dome at the front, slightly bulging.
▶ Middle layer (vascular tunic): add the choroid lining the inside of the back of the eye (use a dark color). Build the ciliary body as a ring just behind where the cornea ends. Add the iris as a colored ring with a hole in the middle (the pupil).
▶ Lens: add a transparent biconvex disk just behind the iris/pupil, suspended by thin lines (the suspensory ligaments) from the ciliary body.
▶ Inner layer (retina): coat the inside of the back of the eyeball with a thin layer in a third color — this is the retina. Mark a small bright spot in the center (the fovea) and a small dark spot where the optic nerve exits (the optic disc / blind spot).
▶ Optic nerve: draw a thick cable emerging from the back of the eyeball at the optic disc. Label it “CN II.”
Label all parts: cornea, sclera, iris, pupil, ciliary body, lens, choroid, retina, fovea, optic disc, optic nerve. Keep the page.
