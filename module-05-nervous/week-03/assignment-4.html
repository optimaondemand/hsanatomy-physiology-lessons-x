📖 READING FOCUS
Reading 5.12 — Touch, the Somatosensory System, and Brain-Body Integration
The substantive content for this assignment lives on the paired Reading page in Canvas.
Before completing this assignment, read Reading 5.12 and complete Lesson 5.12, including the module-end reassembly.
Lesson focus: A single touch signal travels through every layer of the nervous system you have built this module — the perfect synthesis.
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
This is the synthesis assignment for the module. You will trace a single touch signal from the fingertip to the primary somatosensory cortex, naming every structure along the way and tying together what you have built across all three weeks. You also explain the somatosensory homunculus.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
Why is so much of the somatosensory cortex devoted to such small parts of the body?
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
Five submodalities chart. In your notebook, write a five-row chart: touch / pressure, temperature, pain, proprioception, itch. For each, name one specific receptor type (e.g., Pacinian corpuscle, nociceptor, muscle spindle). Photograph and submit.
2
Pathway trace (numbered). A right index fingertip presses against a smooth surface. Write a numbered, ordered list of every structure the touch signal passes through to reach conscious awareness. Aim for at least 9 named structures: fingertip mechanoreceptor → sensory neuron axon → median nerve → dorsal root → dorsal root ganglion (sensory cell body) → dorsal column of spinal cord → medulla (where the dorsal column-medial lemniscus pathway crosses) → medial lemniscus → thalamus (ventral posterior nucleus) → primary somatosensory cortex of the left parietal lobe.
3
Homunculus paragraph (4–6 sentences). Engage the focus question. Explain in your own words why the lips and fingertips take up so much of the somatosensory cortex while the trunk and legs take up so little. Connect cortical area to receptor density.
4
Total length: chart + numbered trace + 100–175 word paragraph. Submit as Canvas text entry with image attachment, or as .docx / .pdf with image embedded.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
Chart names all five submodalities with one specific receptor each.
☐
Pathway trace has at least 9 ordered, correctly named structures — including the crossing and the thalamus.
☐
Homunculus paragraph explicitly links cortical area to receptor density.
