Module thread — Courage. Hearing built the human voice. Smell built memory. Balance built standing upright. Three channels into the brain, three channels into who you are.
📖 TODAY’S FOCUS
Reading 5.11 — The Ear, Taste, and Smell
The substantive content for this lesson lives on the paired Reading page in Canvas.
Before you start the activities below, open Reading 5.11 and read it end-to-end, including the cochlear-implant callout.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to name the three regions of the ear and the major structures in each, and trace the path of a sound wave through them.
2
I will be able to distinguish the cochlea (hearing) from the vestibular apparatus (balance), both in the inner ear.
3
I will be able to name the five primary tastes and the two cranial nerves that carry taste from the tongue.
4
I will be able to describe the unusual feature of olfaction — that it is the only sense that does not relay through the thalamus.

🥽 YOUR VR SESSION THIS WEEK
This session sculpts one ear at exaggerated scale (off-body). The ear is a required structure in your module-end snapshot.
▶ Outer ear. Sculpt a stylized pinna on the outside, and behind it an angled cylinder for the external auditory canal. At the inner end of the canal, place a thin disc for the tympanic membrane.
▶ Middle ear. Behind the tympanic membrane, sculpt the three ossicles in their chain: malleus attached to the tympanic membrane, incus in the middle, stapes pressing on the oval window. Add a small tube heading down and inward — the auditory (eustachian) tube.
▶ Inner ear. Beyond the oval window, sculpt the snail-shell shape of the cochlea. Above and behind it, sculpt the three semicircular canals in three different planes, plus two small bulges representing the utricle and saccule.
▶ Vestibulocochlear nerve. Sculpt a cable emerging from both the cochlea and the vestibular structures, leaving the inner ear. Label it “CN VIII.”
Label every part: pinna, external auditory canal, tympanic membrane, malleus, incus, stapes, oval window, auditory tube, cochlea, semicircular canals, utricle, saccule, vestibulocochlear nerve (CN VIII). Save the file.
