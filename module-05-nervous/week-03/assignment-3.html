📖 READING FOCUS
Reading 5.11 — The Ear, Taste, and Smell
The substantive content for this assignment lives on the paired Reading page in Canvas.
Before completing this assignment, read Reading 5.11 and complete Lesson 5.11, including the VR ear sculpt.
Lesson focus: The ear handles two senses at once; the chemical senses (taste and smell) are simpler structurally but feed important parts of the brain.
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
This assignment has three parts: your labeled VR ear sculpt, a recorded narration tracing a sound wave's path through the ear, and short summaries of taste and smell.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
Why does losing your sense of smell sometimes precede other neurological symptoms in diseases like Parkinson’s and Alzheimer’s?
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
Labeled VR ear sculpt. Submit a screenshot of your completed VR ear sculpt from Lesson 5.11. Required labels: pinna, external auditory canal, tympanic membrane, malleus, incus, stapes, oval window, auditory tube, cochlea, semicircular canals, utricle, saccule, vestibulocochlear nerve.
2
Recorded sound-path narration. Submit a 60–90 second audio recording (a written narration is an acceptable substitute only if a recording device is genuinely unavailable) tracing a sound wave from the air to nerve impulses leaving the ear, naming each structure in order as the wave passes through it: pinna → external auditory canal → tympanic membrane → malleus → incus → stapes → oval window → fluid wave in cochlea → hair cells in organ of Corti → vestibulocochlear nerve (CN VIII). At least 10 named structures, in order.
3
Taste and smell summary (6–9 sentences). Briefly describe (a) the five basic tastes and which two cranial nerves carry taste signals from the tongue, and (b) why olfaction is anatomically unusual among the senses. Engage the focus question.
4
Total submission: VR sculpt screenshot + audio recording (or written narration) + 150–225 word summary. Submit as a Canvas text entry with the screenshot and audio file attached, or as a single .docx / .pdf with the screenshot embedded and a link to the audio file.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
All 13 ear labels correctly placed in their proper region (outer / middle / inner).
☐
Narration names at least 10 structures in the correct order, spoken from memory (not read from a list).
☐
Summary names all 5 tastes, identifies CN VII and IX, and explains the thalamus-bypass of olfaction and its proximity to limbic structures.
