🥽 YOUR VR SESSION THIS WEEK
This session has two parts: an off-body cross-section sculpt and a refinement to the figure’s spinal cord.
▶ Part 1 — Off-body cross-section (~20 min). In open space, sculpt a single transverse cross-section of the spinal cord at exaggerated scale, as if you cut through it and were looking at the cut face. Draw the H-shaped (butterfly) gray matter in one color, with all four named regions (right and left dorsal horns, right and left ventral horns; add lateral horns as small bulges between them). Add the central canal as a small dot in the middle. Surround the gray matter with white matter in a second color. Show one set of dorsal and ventral roots emerging from one side, merging into a single spinal nerve. Mark the dorsal root ganglion as a small swelling on the dorsal root.
▶ Part 2 — On-figure refinement (~10 min). On your body figure, identify the level of the spinal cord at which an injury would knock out the diaphragm. (Answer: C3–C5.) Place a small marker at that level on the cord. Place a second marker where the cord itself ends in adults (between L1 and L2).
Label everything in the cross-section. Save both files. The cross-section is one of the required off-body details for your module-end snapshot.
