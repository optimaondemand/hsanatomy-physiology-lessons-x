✏️ YOUR DRAWING SESSION THIS WEEK
This session refines the brain page from Lesson 5.05. You will add the four lobes onto the cerebrum, then add the ventricular system and the three meninges. This is your most layered drawing session of the module — take your time, and use a different colored pencil for each layer.
▶ Add the four lobes. Take out your color-coded brain page. On the cerebrum, sub-divide the surface into frontal, parietal, temporal, and occipital lobes — using the central sulcus and lateral sulcus as the main landmarks. Use four distinct shades. Label each lobe and at least one specialized cortex area in each (e.g., primary motor in frontal, primary somatosensory in parietal, primary auditory in temporal, primary visual in occipital). Mark Broca’s area in the left frontal lobe.
▶ Add the ventricular system. Use dashed outlines, as if looking through the cerebrum. Inside the brain, mark the two lateral ventricles (one in each hemisphere), the third ventricle in the diencephalon, and the fourth ventricle between brainstem and cerebellum. Show that the fourth ventricle is continuous with the central canal of the spinal cord.
▶ Add the meninges. Wrap the brain in three concentric layers (use a small magnified inset at the edge of the page to show the layers clearly). From outside in: dura mater (thick, opaque), arachnoid mater (web-like, lighter), pia mater (very thin, adhering to every fold of the cortex). Label each.
Keep this page. This is the brain page you will include in your end-of-module Assembled Figure.
