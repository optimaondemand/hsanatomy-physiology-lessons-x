📖 READING FOCUS
Reading 5.08 — Cranial and Spinal Nerves
The substantive content for this assignment lives on the paired Reading page in Canvas.
Before completing this assignment, read Reading 5.08 and complete Lesson 5.08, including the cranial / spinal nerve drawings.
Lesson focus: The 12 cranial nerves and the major spinal plexuses are the wiring diagram of the PNS — clinically essential to localize injuries.
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
This assignment has three parts: a cranial-nerve infographic with your own memory mnemonic, a labeled drawing of cranial nerves at their brainstem origins, and a clinical-localization paragraph about a specific deficit.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
Given a patient’s symptom, how can a clinician reason backward to the specific nerve that has been damaged?
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
Cranial-nerve infographic. Submit your one-page infographic highlighting 2 purely sensory, 2 purely motor, and 2 mixed cranial nerves (Roman numeral, name, and primary function for each), plus a mnemonic sentence you wrote yourself for remembering all 12 in order. Photograph or scan it.
2
Cranial-nerve drawing — cranial nerves at brainstem. Submit a photo (or digital export) of your drawing showing the underside of the brain with at least 8 of the 12 cranial nerves labeled at their brainstem origins.
3
Localization paragraph (6–8 sentences). For three of the symptoms below, identify the most likely damaged nerve and a one-sentence justification:

(a) Loss of smell after a head injury.
(b) Right-sided facial drooping with intact sensation.
(c) Inability to abduct the left eye (the eye won’t turn outward).
(d) Numbness on the lateral foot.
(e) Hoarseness and difficulty swallowing after thyroid surgery.

Engage the focus question in your final sentence.
4
Total length: infographic + cranial-nerve drawing + 200–300 words. Submit as Canvas text entry with image attachments, or as .docx / .pdf with images embedded.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
Infographic correctly sorts 2 sensory, 2 motor, and 2 mixed cranial nerves, and includes a self-written mnemonic covering all 12 in order.
☐
Cranial-nerve drawing shows at least 8 of 12 cranial nerves correctly labeled at their brainstem origins.
☐
All three chosen symptoms localized to the correct nerve with a specific justification (key answers: a = CN I; b = CN VII; c = CN VI; d = S1; e = CN X / recurrent laryngeal branch).
