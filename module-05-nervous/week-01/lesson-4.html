🥽 YOUR VR SESSION THIS WEEK
This VR session is a tracing session on the figure. You will draw the full path of a withdrawal reflex directly onto the body, from a fingertip back to a flexor muscle of the arm.
What to do. Open ENGAGE. Load the figure with the muscle layer visible (carry forward from Module 4 if you can; otherwise load the male figure with the upper limb muscles visible).
▶ Place a marker at the tip of the index finger of the right hand — this is your sensory receptor (a nociceptor). Label it.
▶ Draw a line from the fingertip up the median nerve in the arm, then in to the spinal cord at approximately the C6–C8 level (in the lower cervical region). This is your sensory neuron.
▶ Inside the spinal cord, mark a small junction representing the integration center — one or more interneurons.
▶ Draw a second line from the integration center out through a spinal nerve at the same cervical level, down to the biceps brachii. This is your motor neuron.
▶ Mark the biceps brachii as your effector. (Optionally also draw an inhibitory branch to the triceps, showing it would be inhibited.)
Label each segment with which component it represents (sensory receptor / sensory neuron / integration center / motor neuron / effector). Add an arrow showing the direction of travel along each segment.
This is not an off-body detail. This tracing is on the figure and demonstrates how a reflex circuit overlays the anatomy you already built.
