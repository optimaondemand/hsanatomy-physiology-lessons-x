Module thread — Courage. The cells of the nervous system are charged batteries. Every signal you send is a discharge of stored work. Be impressed by what your body spends to keep you ready to think.

🥽 YOUR VR SESSION THIS WEEK
This is your first sculpting session of Module 5. You will sculpt one neuron at exaggerated scale (off-body, in open space). This neuron is one of the “off-body detail” structures required in your end-of-module snapshot.
What to do. Open ENGAGE. In an open area — not on the body figure — use the 3D pen tool to sculpt a single neuron in this order:
▶ Draw a roughly egg-shaped cell body (soma) in the center of your workspace. Mark a small spot inside it for the nucleus.
▶ From one side of the cell body, draw 5–8 short, branching dendrites. These should look bushy and tapered toward the tips.
▶ From the opposite side of the cell body, draw a small bump (the axon hillock), then extend a single, long, smooth line outward for the axon. Make it noticeably longer than the dendrites — an arm’s length or so — to show that real axons can run far from the cell body.
▶ Along the axon, sculpt 3–5 rounded segments of myelin sheath, with small unwrapped gaps between them. These gaps are the nodes of Ranvier.
▶ At the far end of the axon, draw a small branching cluster of axon terminals.
Label every part: dendrites, cell body, nucleus, axon hillock, axon, myelin sheath, node of Ranvier, axon terminals. Add an arrow showing signal direction (dendrites → cell body → axon → terminals).
Save your work. You will refine and re-use this neuron in Lesson 5.03 when you sculpt a synapse onto its terminals.
