📖 READING FOCUS
Reading 5.01 — Overview of the Nervous System
The substantive content for this assignment lives on the paired Reading page in Canvas.
Before completing this assignment, read Reading 5.01 and complete the activities in Lesson 5.01, including the Nervous System Map orientation.
Lesson focus: The nervous system is one continuous structure; everything in this module starts by being able to draw it and label its pieces.
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
This assignment has two parts. First, a labeled CNS/PNS body map showing the central nervous system and three representative peripheral nerves. Second, a short labeled-neuron sketch with a written paragraph that explains, in your own words, the difference between somatic and autonomic activity. These are the foundation drawings you will rely on for the next three weeks.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
Why does the nervous system have separate somatic and autonomic divisions — and what would the body do badly if it only had one?
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
CNS / PNS body map. Submit a photograph or scan of your hand-drawn body map from the lesson activity. The map must clearly show: (a) the brain and spinal cord (CNS), (b) at least one cranial nerve, (c) at least two spinal nerves at different spinal levels, and (d) one peripheral target labeled for each (face, diaphragm, leg, etc.). Use two different colors for CNS and PNS.
2
Labeled neuron sketch. Submit a photograph or scan of your labeled neuron sketch. All seven parts must be labeled: dendrites, cell body (soma), axon hillock, axon, myelin sheath, node of Ranvier, axon terminals. An arrow must show the direction of signal travel.
3
Somatic vs autonomic paragraph (5–8 sentences). Answer the focus question. Define somatic and autonomic, give one specific everyday example of each, and explain why the body needs both. Do not restate the question.
4
Total length: two diagrams + 150–250 words of writing. Submit as Canvas text entry with image attachments, or as a .docx / .pdf with the images embedded.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
CNS and PNS visibly distinguished by color; at least one cranial and two spinal nerves drawn with destinations labeled.
☐
All seven neuron parts labeled and correctly placed; arrow shows correct signal direction.
☐
Somatic and autonomic correctly defined with specific (not generic) examples.
☐
Paragraph engages the focus question with a real argument, not a restated definition.
