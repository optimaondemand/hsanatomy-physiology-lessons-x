S1 Review Lesson — The Body in Support, Movement, Regulation, and Transport

📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
Fill in the six-row comparative chart below, one row per Semester 1 organ system. Then write a short reflection paragraph at the end. The chart is designed to make you say in your words what each system does, what tissue it is made of, where it meets another system, and which Optima virtue it has illustrated this semester.
Most cells need a phrase or one short sentence — not a paragraph. The reflection paragraph at the end is where you can stretch.

🧠 HOW TO ANSWER EACH COLUMN
Main function. One short phrase that captures what this system primarily does for the organism. (Example for the lymphatic system, if you had to do it: "returns interstitial fluid to the blood and houses the immune cells.")
Key tissue type(s). Name the tissue type(s) from Module 2 that this system is mostly made of. For most systems you will name 1–2 of the four (epithelial, connective, muscle, nervous). Some systems — especially the integument — use almost all four.
Primary cross-connection. Name one other Semester 1 system this one cannot function without, and say in 1–2 sentences how they interact. Do not repeat the same pairing in two different rows — use a different connection for each system.
One virtue this system illustrates. The Module’s confirmed virtue thread is fair game (Wonder, Perseverance, Self-government, Courage, Courtesy, Service). You may pick a different virtue from Optima’s seven (courage, courtesy, honesty, perseverance, self-government, service, responsibility) if you can defend it in your reflection.
✏️ REFLECTION PARAGRAPH (REQUIRED)
After your chart, write a 120–200 word reflection that answers all three of these prompts in one continuous paragraph:
A
Pick one cross-connection from your chart and explain why it is more interesting than the others — what would happen if it failed?
B
Pick one virtue you assigned and defend it in one sentence. Why does this system — specifically — teach that virtue?
C
Finish with one sentence on the claim from the Review Lesson: the body is best understood as one organism, not six systems. Agree or disagree, briefly.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐

☐

☐
Reflection paragraph is 120–200 words and answers all three prompts (A, B, C).
☐
Reflection uses specific anatomical or physiological terms — not just feeling-language.
