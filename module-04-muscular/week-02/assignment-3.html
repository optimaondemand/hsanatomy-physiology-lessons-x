Lesson focus: Three crossed fiber directions plus a midline strap make the abdominal wall strong from every angle.
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
Third atlas assignment of Week 2. Same shape as the previous two. The focus question this time is a cross-system synthesis (cough mechanics) that uses muscles from both Lesson 4.06 and Lesson 4.07.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
Which muscles fire during a hard cough, and in what order?
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
OIA + fiber-direction table. Four rows: rectus abdominis, external oblique, internal oblique, transversus abdominis. Include the fiber direction column.
2
Labeled Muscle Map photo. Front view of your Muscle Map showing all four abdominal muscles in their colors, with the linea alba marked. The fiber directions should be visually distinguishable.
3
Cough sequence (5–7 sentences). Answer the focus question. Walk through a hard cough in three steps: (a) the inhale that precedes the cough, (b) the glottis closes briefly while the abdominal wall and internal intercostals contract together, (c) the glottis opens and air rushes out under pressure. Name at least four muscles by name from the past two lessons.
4
Total length: OIA table + Muscle Map photo + 200–300 words.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
All four OIA rows accurate; fiber direction column complete.
☐
Muscle Map photo shows all four muscles + linea alba; fiber directions distinguishable.
☐
Cough sequence names at least four muscles by name, including both abdominal and thoracic muscles.
