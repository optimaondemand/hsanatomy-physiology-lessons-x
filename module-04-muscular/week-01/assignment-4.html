📖 READING FOCUS
Reading 4.04 — The Neuromuscular Junction
The substantive content for this assignment lives on the paired Reading page in Canvas.
Before completing this assignment, read Reading 4.04 and complete the activities in Lesson 4.04, including the VR NMJ sculpt.
Lesson focus: The signal from a motor neuron crosses to a muscle fiber as a chemical — acetylcholine — not as electricity.
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
This assignment closes Week 1. You will (1) annotate or label your VR sculpt of the NMJ, (2) write the 6-step transmission script in order, and (3) compare two specific NMJ poisons or medicines (botulinum toxin vs. an acetylcholinesterase inhibitor) by what step of the cycle each one acts on.
🎯 FOCUS QUESTION
Two different toxins can act on the neuromuscular junction. Why do they produce opposite kinds of muscle problems?
📝 STUDENT DIRECTIONS
1
NMJ diagram with all seven labels. Submit a photograph or VR snapshot of your sculpted NMJ. Confirm that all seven labels from the lesson are placed: axon terminal, synaptic vesicle, ACh in cleft, synaptic cleft, motor end plate, ACh receptor, Na⁺ entry arrow.
2
Six-step transmission script. Write the six steps of transmission at the NMJ in order, each in one sentence. Use the words action potential, calcium, vesicle, acetylcholine, receptor, end-plate potential, and acetylcholinesterase at least once each. Numbered list.
3
Two-toxin comparison (one paragraph). Answer the focus question by comparing botulinum toxin (which blocks vesicle fusion and prevents ACh release) with an acetylcholinesterase inhibitor (which blocks the breakdown of ACh in the synaptic cleft). For each, name the specific step from your 6-step script that is affected, and describe the muscle problem that results (paralysis vs. continuous contraction / spasm). 4–6 sentences.
4
One-sentence Loewi note. In one sentence, state in your own words what Otto Loewi’s 1921 frog-heart experiment demonstrated about how nerves communicate with organs.
5
Total length: diagram + 250–400 words. Submit as Canvas text entry with image attachment, or as a single .docx / .pdf with the image embedded.
✅ SUCCESS CRITERIA
☐
NMJ diagram includes all seven labels in correct positions.
☐
Six transmission steps in correct order; all seven required words appear.
☐
Two-toxin comparison correctly attributes paralysis to botulinum (no ACh release) and spasm to AChE inhibitor (ACh persists in cleft).
☐
Loewi note captures the “chemical, not purely electrical” idea in the student’s own words.
