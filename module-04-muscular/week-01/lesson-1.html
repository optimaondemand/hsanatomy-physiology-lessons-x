Module thread — Self-government. One of the three muscle types answers to your conscious will. The other two run on their own. Most of being a healthy adult is knowing which is which, and treating each with the right kind of respect.

🥽 YOUR VR SESSION THIS WEEK
This week is an orientation session, not a sculpting session. You will use VR to locate a sample of each of the three muscle types in the body, in their natural location, so that next week when you start sculpting skeletal muscle you have a sense of where you are headed.
What to do. Open ENGAGE and load HoloAnatomy (male figure — the course default for Modules 3–11). Carry forward the assembled skeleton you submitted at the end of Module 3 if your environment supports it; otherwise reload the standard figure.
▶ Skeletal muscle — the limbs. Find any one major muscle of the upper or lower limb (the biceps brachii on the front of the upper arm is easy to spot). Notice that it is long, parallel-fibered, and attaches to two bones via tendons. Place a label.
▶ Smooth muscle — the gut. Look inside the abdominal cavity. Identify the stomach wall and the small intestine wall. The muscle inside their walls is smooth muscle. You will not be sculpting it — just locate it and place a label.
▶ Cardiac muscle — the heart. Locate the heart in the thoracic cavity. The entire muscular wall of the heart (the myocardium) is cardiac muscle. Place a label.
Before you leave the environment, do a 90-second mental tour: walk around the figure once and name each muscle type as you pass its location. (“Biceps — skeletal. Stomach wall — smooth. Heart — cardiac.”) Hand-on-bone is the habit you built in Module 3; eyes-and-name is the habit for this module’s first session.
Time: ~20 minutes.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to name the three types of muscle tissue and give one location in the body where each is found.
2
I will be able to compare the three muscle types on striation, voluntary control, cell shape, and number of nuclei.
3
I will be able to explain why cardiac muscle’s intercalated discs let the heart contract as a single coordinated unit.
4
I will be able to classify a body movement (running, swallowing, heartbeat, blink, intestinal contraction) as driven by skeletal, smooth, or cardiac muscle.
