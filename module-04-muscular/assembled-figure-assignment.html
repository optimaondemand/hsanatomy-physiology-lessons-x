The Assembled Muscular System
Figure submission · 25 pts · Due end of Module 4 (Week 3)
Module thread — Self-government. The full muscular layer of the figure you have built is what every voluntary motion of a healthy body runs through. Submit the body you would meet in any anatomical study.
📋 ASSIGNMENT OVERVIEW
During this module you drew muscles lesson by lesson onto your Muscle Map (front and back body outlines, with your Module 3 Skeleton Atlas as a guide to the bones), and you drew detail pages for the muscle cross-section, the sarcomere, and the neuromuscular junction. For this assignment, you gather all of your work into a single complete muscular figure, photograph (or export) it, and submit it along with progress images and a brief reflection caption. This is your record of having built the muscular system layer by layer over three weeks — not a perfect textbook diagram, but the figure you made. Paper is highly recommended (draw by hand, lay the pages out together, photograph in good light); a digital drawing assembled on one slide is also accepted.
🧸 EXPECTED STRUCTURES IN YOUR FIGURE
Your figure must show the major skeletal muscles drawn on your front and back body outlines, plus at least one detail page. Specifically:
Head and neck (8 muscles)
▶
Masseter, temporalis, sternocleidomastoid, orbicularis oculi, orbicularis oris, frontalis, zygomaticus major, buccinator.
Back
▶
Trapezius, latissimus dorsi, erector spinae group (with the three columns — iliocostalis, longissimus, spinalis — identifiable, e.g. through a cut-away window or labels).
Thorax
▶
External + internal intercostals (with fiber directions visibly different); diaphragm with its three openings labeled.
Abdomen
▶
Rectus abdominis, external oblique, internal oblique, transversus abdominis, linea alba.
Upper limb (at least one side complete)
▶
Deltoid, biceps brachii, triceps brachii, forearm flexors (anterior compartment), forearm extensors (posterior compartment).
Lower limb (at least one side complete)
▶
Gluteus maximus, quadriceps femoris (all four heads identifiable), hamstrings (three muscles identifiable), gastrocnemius, soleus.
Detail page (required — at least one)
▶
A sarcomere from Lesson 4.03 (with Z-discs, thin filaments, thick filaments, A-band, I-band, H-zone, M-line labeled) and/or a neuromuscular junction from Lesson 4.04 (with axon terminal, vesicles, synaptic cleft, motor end plate, ACh receptors labeled). At least one of these must be present; including both is encouraged.
✏️ REFLECTION CAPTION (50–100 words)
Record yourself speaking a caption of 50–100 words (about 30–50 seconds) with your phone, laptop, or the Canvas media recorder; a written caption is accepted only if you genuinely cannot record. Your caption should:
1. Name what you drew (broad regions + your detail page).
2. Identify one substantive thing you learned about how the muscular system works — not just what it looks like. (Example: “I had not appreciated until I drew the diaphragm with its three openings how much of breathing depends on the geometry of a single muscle.”)
Attach three dated progress images — one page from each week (for example, from Lessons 4.03, 4.06, and 4.09).
